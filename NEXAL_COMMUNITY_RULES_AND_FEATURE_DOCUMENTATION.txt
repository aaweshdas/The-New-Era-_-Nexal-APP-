SECTION 1 - WHAT YOU ARE ALLOWED TO DO (PERMITTED ACTIVITIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1  CREATE A PERSONAL PROFILE
     You may set up your own Nexal account with a verified Gmail address, custom
     display name, creative @username handle, profile picture, location, and bio.

1.2  POST ORIGINAL CONTENT
     You may share original photos, videos, text posts, polls, audio tracks, and
     creative work. Express yourself freely while respecting community standards.

1.3  FOLLOW AND INTERACT WITH OTHERS
     You may follow other users, like posts (with heart animations), leave comments,
     share content, and bookmark/save posts to your private vault.

1.4  CONTROL YOUR PRIVACY
     You may toggle your account to Public or Private mode, restrict direct messages,
     hide online status, block users, and manage biometric & encryption settings.

1.5  REPORT VIOLATIONS
     You may use the built-in report feature to flag inappropriate posts, comments,
     or accounts. Reports are reviewed by the moderation team within 24 hours.

1.6  SHARE OTHERS' CONTENT WITH CREDIT
     You may repost or share content created by other users as long as proper credit
     is given to the original creator and it respects content guidelines.

1.7  HONEST BUSINESS PROMOTION
     You may share products, commercial services, portfolios, or creative work.
     Promotional content is permitted as long as it is honest, non-deceptive, and not spam.

1.8  LIVE STREAMING & REEL CREATION
     You may broadcast live streams and publish vertical Reels (Feels) as long as
     the live broadcast and recorded media follow community guidelines.

1.9  PARTICIPATE IN DISCUSSIONS & CYBER QUESTS
     You may participate in public feed discussions, community polls, AI prompts,
     and open-world arcade quests in a respectful and fair manner.

1.10 USE ALL PLATFORM FEATURES
     You may utilize all native Nexal features including Stories, Feels (Reels),
     Long-Form Videos, Polls, Direct Messages, Socket.IO Voice/Video Calls,
     ARIA AI Assistant, Arcade Games, Media Gallery, and Spatial Radar Maps.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 - WHAT IS STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED (PROHIBITED CONDUCT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1  HARASSMENT AND BULLYING
     Do NOT target, threaten, humiliate, stalk, or harass any individual or group
     through messages, comments, feed posts, or live streams.

2.2  IMPERSONATION AND FAKE IDENTITIES
     Do NOT create accounts pretending to be another real person, celebrity, brand,
     official Nexal administrator, or the ARIA AI system.

2.3  SPREADING MISINFORMATION
     Do NOT deliberately post fake news, fabricated medical advice, manipulated stats,
     or misleading information that causes real-world harm.

2.4  HATE SPEECH AND DISCRIMINATION
     Do NOT post content promoting hatred, violence, or discrimination based on race,
     ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.

2.5  BOT ACTIVITY, SPAM & API EXPLOITS
     Do NOT use automated bots, scripts, scrapers, or third-party tools to mass-follow,
     spam DMs/comments, manipulate likes, or exploit the API Gateway.

2.6  HACKING AND UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS
     Do NOT attempt to access another user's account, bypass 6-digit OTP verification,
     tamper with database tables, or launch Denial-of-Service attacks.

2.7  SCAMS AND FRAUDULENT ACTIVITY
     Do NOT engage in phishing, fraudulent giveaways, pyramid schemes, or deceptive
     practices intended to steal user data, credentials, or funds.

2.8  SELF-HARM AND DANGEROUS CONTENT
     Do NOT share, promote, or glorify self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or
     dangerous viral stunts that risk human life.

2.9  THREATS AND VIOLENT CONTENT
     Do NOT make threats of physical violence against any person or group, or post
     content inciting violence or civil unrest.

2.10 DOXXING AND PRIVACY INVASION
     Do NOT post another individual's private information (home address, phone number,
     workplace, financial data, or legal IDs) without explicit written consent.

2.11 UNDERAGE EXPLOITATION (CSAM)
     Do NOT share, create, or distribute any content that exploits or endangers minors.
     Child abuse material results in immediate reporting to federal authorities.

2.12 MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS TO BYPASS BANS
     Do NOT register new accounts or use alternate Gmail addresses to evade an active
     suspension or permanent ban. Ban evasion triggers immediate IP and Device locks.

3.9  Extremism & Terrorism: Propaganda, recruitment, or glorification of extremist groups.

SECTION 4 - RULE ENFORCEMENT, STRIKE SYSTEM & BAN POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- ZERO TOLERANCE OFFENSES (IMMEDIATE PERMANENT BAN) ---
The following violations result in an IMMEDIATE PERMANENT BAN with no warning and no appeal:
  1. Sharing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  2. Credible threats of physical violence or terrorism.
  3. Large-scale platform hacking, fraud, or server attacks.
  4. Attempting to bypass a prior permanent ban (Ban Evasion).

--- 4-STRIKE ENFORCEMENT SYSTEM (STANDARD VIOLATIONS) ---
  - STRIKE 1 (Formal Warning):
    Violating content is permanently deleted. User receives an official in-app notice.
    No feature restrictions applied. Account remains in good standing.
  - STRIKE 2 (Temporary Restriction - 7 to 30 Days):
    User loses ability to create posts, upload reels, comment, or send DMs for 7-30 days.
    Profile remains visible, but read-only mode is enforced.
  - STRIKE 3 (90-Day Account Suspension):
    Account is fully suspended for 90 days. Profile and content are hidden from search.
    User receives final notice prior to potential reactivation.
  - STRIKE 4 (Permanent Ban & Data Purge):
    Account is permanently deactivated. All profile data, posts, messages, and scores are
    purged after 30 days. IP address, hardware ID, and Gmail address are permanently blacklisted.

--- APPEAL RIGHTS ---
  - Appeals for Strike 1, 2, and 3 must be submitted within 14 days of notice.
  - Appeals are reviewed by the Moderation Panel within 5 to 7 business days.
  - Zero-Tolerance bans (CSAM, Hacking, Threats) are non-appealable.

--- STRIKE RESET POLICY ---
  - Accounts maintaining 12 consecutive months of good standing without new violations
    qualify for a clean record review, resetting previous strikes.

SECTION 5 - PRIVACY, DATA PROTECTION & UNCONSENTED RECORDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Data Harvesting: Users must not harvest, scrape, or collect personal data of other users.
5.2 Unconsented Recordings: Users must not screenshot or record private DM conversations,
    voice calls, or video calls to publish them publicly without explicit consent of all parties.
5.3 Unauthorized Tracking: Using Map proximity features or third-party tools to stalk or
    track individuals without their knowledge is strictly forbidden.
5.4 Data Governance: All stored data is encrypted and protected under Nexal's Privacy Policy.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 - POLICY UPDATES & NOTIFICATION TERMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Major updates to platform rules will be communicated at least 7 days prior to taking effect
    via in-app notification and registered email.
6.2 Continued platform usage after updates take effect constitutes full acceptance of terms.
